Chapter 26 - ENERGY (Jeff)

Many years ago, I attended a workshop at a conference that a friend was taking about how to find new clients in your local area. His advertising strategy was simple; Health, Energy and Fitness. Buy them via Google AdWords and turn it up to 11. The word Energy had a point. What most people want is enough energy to do things. To actually feel like they aren’t moving through sludge to do even the things they enjoy. Strength and Fitness are one thing, but if you can’t get out of bed most days without feeling like you want to hop back in, why focus on adding another 5kgs to your bench press?

Energy is subjective. Feeling tired has become fashionable, and in my part of the world almost a greeting alongside “Good thanks how are you?”. You might be tired, but are you good?

Energy comes from being well rested, eating well (whatever that means for you, yet another lifelong process), staying hydrated and doing other daily boring stuff which isn’t going to give you much of a dopamine hit.

You might have entertained (or been told via a wellness influencer) that you have parasites, S.A.D. (Seasonal Affective Disorder), un aligned chakras, carbohydrate intolerence, toe cancer, lack of fiber, caffiene overwhelm or the moons are in the wrong position. Some of these could be true, hopefully not all of them.

My coaching with some of my clients around may revolve around the same challenge for months, even years. Getting to bed early.

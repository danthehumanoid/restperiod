Chapter 15 - SHOW ME THE RESULTS!

Whenever I see yet another video of a coach or trainer online saying something along the lines of “I’ve worked with hundreds and even thousands of people to help them achieve their results”, my bullshit detector starts ringing.

This phrase is almost so cliche that they may as well skip straight to the line of “like comment and subscribe”.

I say this from my own personal experience of working with what would be hundreds of people, thousands if I count the group fitness classes I taught.

And barely any of them are with me today, nor did they get the results they probably wanted. More recently (the past 6-7 years) I’ve been a lot more consistent with helping people achieve what they want.

But honestly, if I wanted to show you the results of clients I’ve personally worked with one on one, week to week, achieving lifelong transformations and results, it would probably be less than 50 people.

It’s definitely not thousands. Life is complicated, training is complicated and maininting habits is hard.

Some people moved on, some people gave up, I gave up on some people and some people only trained with me for 3 months 10 years ago.

I have no idea how hundreds of them are doing now. Although I do know that a few died (not not my watch!)

If I count results being, I had people turn up to a Group Fitness class, then I take back everything I said about not helping 1000s of people achieving their lifelong fitness goals.

Asking any coach for testimonials and honest results of clients is reasonable. You could ask to see examples of training programs, testing protocols, goals set and achieved or just ask to talk to a client personally.

I’d like to think that taking on a client that has been spending money on things that don’t work for years, coming to see me or someone who I recommend would be much more of a time saving expenditure versus spending yet “more money.

A coach can never guarantee results. But a great coach can increase the chances Like a bottle of hand sanitiser that kills 99.99% of germs. Results come from doing the work (and washing your hands for 20 seconds).

Sometimes a coach and client relationship doesn’t work out and it’s time to move on.

That's better for both people and it should be a celebration rather than feeling like you’ve been dumped by yet another partner.

Hopefully results based becomes the standard in the fitness industry and not something you need to hunt for.

So for now, you’ll just have to politely shout “SHOW ME THE RESULTS!”

Principe of Individuality

There are one of three things we are doing we training/exercising - Recovering, Maintaining or Aiming to improve. If we simply do no trainining i.e. don't apply any stimulis to our bodies, anything we have gained (muscle emass, strength, bone density, cardiovascualr health) starts to regress and decline. And this all happens at different rates. We loose our ability to produce power/speed very quickly (within days) an strength, muscle an aerobic fitness over a slightly longer period of time. Therefore if someone is training for mutliple skills/biomotor profiles, they need to ensure that they are consistnely dosing each profile frequenyl enough to either continue improbing or at least maintain

Whilst on a holiday in Bali, I opted to do my traditional “tourist run”. This consisted of half mapping out a 8-10km run picking a direction and run somewhere I’d never seen before, always leaving space to stop if I found something interesting. And I always did.

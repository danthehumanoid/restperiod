Snap your punches fast!

Two summers as a kid, I took up boxing. Classic strategic self defence move and yet another way to work on my aerobic fitness with friends. The boxing gym was right next to a Judo hall. And every night I’d walk in to the boxing studio, eyes down and ignore the judo kids as they did the same.

When I played rugby, we always looked at a distance towards the Soccer fields and saw them as “others”, almost making a point never to cross the imaginary line soereting the rugby fields from the soccer pitches. I would go to my sister dancing recitals and watch as two boys amongst 30 girls danced and entertained an entire crowd of adults who were mostly just waiting for their kid to come on stage.

I remember going to a Saturday morning tennis club open day and found myself a little lost playing one on one with just one other person, not a group of people.

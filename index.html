What is Rest Period?

Rest Period is an online exercise and educational resource built by Daniel Hardie — Strength and Conditioning Coach, Mover and Educator with 15 years in the fitness industry.

The aim is simple: more humans moving better, more of the time, safely and enjoyably.

Rest Period includes:

  • A full exercise library with over 1000 video demos
  • Guided warm up, strength, run and mobility programs
  • Session logging — track your weights, reps and progress
  • Science-backed educational resources
  • A philosophy section drawn from real coaching conversations during you guessed it, rest periods
  • Training tools — pace solver, 1RM calculator, interval timer and more

It's free to sign up and always will be. Premium programs are available for $19.99 NZD/month.

As always, consult a medical professional before starting any exercise program.

Chapter 31 - TRAIN, THEN PRACTICE

Outside of the sports powerlifting or olympic lifting or strongmen events have you ever seen an athlete lift 400 kgs whilst competing? Yet why does a rugby player do this?

Or a sprinter? Strength training helps teach our body to organise stress. Like a lighting bolt that finds the most efficient path to ground.

Drills, practice routines and other skill based activities are another session in itself.

Can you combine them? Of course!

But should you completely? I’m not convinced.

Functional patterns is a misnomer, and yet it still sounds trendy. The best results I’ve seen clients and other coaches around me get with their’s is to draw the line. You’re either training or practicing. If you’re an everyday person, you’re either training or practicing.

Do you need to go to the gym, pretend to lift a 10kg dumbbell like a washing basket, carry it 20 meters, put it down and lift it up to put it on a shelf?

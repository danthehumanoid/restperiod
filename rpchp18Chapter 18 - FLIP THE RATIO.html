EXERCISE - PHILOSOPHY: FLIP THE RATIO

When I started playing music again, I also started sharing that I was playing music again.

For the first few months, I had a routine of setting up my laptop, phone, tripod, connecting everything to my instruments until finally I played! … For around 15 minutes until I decided I needed to rearrange the tripod, things got tangled, I pulled out cables, lost my focus and ultimately called it an evening. Almost. I spent around 90 minutes editing footage.

One of my favourite creative projects, bogged down by my wanting to get perfect content.

So I gave up. Not playing music, but recording. I shared nothing but tiny snippets with close friends when I had breakthroughs.

I got better, and I enjoyed it and eventually I found easier ways to record and edit.

Recording the content was never the issue. It was the ratio of playing/producing. I was spending 15 minutes of my time “playing”/practicing and over 2 hours trying to get the perfect video.

Who was I doing this for?

Many people want to share their progress for a variety of reasons; accountability, connecting and of course fame. And maybe it helps. And maybe it also distracts you from the real task at hand. Perhaps you need content for a sponsor, job application or project.

But does the ratio workout for you? Simply, how much time are you spending training, exercising and enjoying your practice just for you?

Go to the gym without your phone sometime.

Take a run without your watch for once.

And never, EVER take a video of a fireworks show ever again.
